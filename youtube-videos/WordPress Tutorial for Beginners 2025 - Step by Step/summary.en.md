## Difference Between WordPress.com and WordPress.org
* **Summary**: WordPress.org offers free, open-source software for self-hosted sites with full access to plugins and themes. WordPress.com is a paid hosting service with limited features in lower plans, requiring upgrades to $40/month for full ecosystem access.
* **Key Takeaway/Example**: Avoid WordPress.com to save money and get unrestricted features; download from .org and host separately.
